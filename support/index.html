Contact support

Email james@pyledesignz.com with a concise description of what happened and what you expected.

Helpful details include:

  • Homestead Companion version and build number;
  • Android version and device model;
  • The screen or feature involved;
  • Steps that reproduce the problem;
  • The exact error message, if any; and
  • For billing only, the RevenueCat support ID displayed in the App.
Protect your records: do not send passwords, payment-card details, complete financial exports, private receipt images, exact farm coordinates, or sensitive animal or household notes unless they are specifically needed to resolve the request.

Purchases, Pro access, and cancellation

I paid but Pro is not active

  1. Confirm the device is online and uses the same Google Play account that made the purchase.
  2. Open the Pro screen and choose Refresh Pro access.
  3. If access is still missing, choose Restore Purchases.
  4. Restart the App and check again.
  5. If the problem continues, email support with the RevenueCat support ID. Do not purchase again while Google Play still shows the subscription as active.

Reinstalling or changing devices

The launch version uses an anonymous RevenueCat app-user identifier. After uninstalling, clearing application storage, or moving to another device, use Restore Purchases while signed into the original Google Play purchasing account. Restoring billing does not restore local farm records; use a JSON backup for supported records.

Canceling

Manage or cancel through Google Play subscriptions. Canceling generally prevents the next renewal while access continues through the paid period. Uninstalling the App or erasing local records does not cancel the subscription.

Refunds and charges

Google Play processes payments and determines refund eligibility. Review the order in Google Play and use Google Play support for payment-method, charge, or refund decisions. PyleDesignz does not receive your full payment-card number.

Local records, exports, and restoration

Homestead Companion stores farm records locally and does not include cloud sync in the initial release. To protect your work:

  • Create periodic full-record JSON exports and keep copies outside the device.
  • Use CSV exports for reporting, not as a complete restorable backup.
  • Keep original receipt images separately; JSON backups do not include the image files.
  • Test that an important backup can be selected and validated before relying on it.

Uninstalling the App, clearing application storage, or resetting or losing the device can remove local records. PyleDesignz cannot retrieve records that existed only on that device.

A restore was rejected

The importer rejects malformed files, unsupported or newer schemas, duplicate identifiers, oversized backups, and invalid record structures. Keep the rejected file unchanged and contact support with the error message. Do not email the backup itself unless support specifically requests it and you have reviewed its contents.

Weather is unavailable or incorrect

Live weather currently uses the U.S. National Weather Service and supports U.S. coordinates. Check that:

  • The device has an internet connection;
  • The farm latitude and longitude are valid;
  • U.S. longitudes are usually negative—for example, Missouri is approximately -92, not 92;
  • The selected coordinates are covered by the National Weather Service; and
  • You retry after a temporary National Weather Service outage.

You can enter coordinates manually without granting device-location permission. Weather and guidance may be delayed or incomplete and should not be your only source for urgent weather decisions.

Scheduled reminders are not appearing

  • Confirm Pro is active for Pro scheduled reminders.
  • Check Android notification permission for Homestead Companion.
  • Review exact-alarm access, battery optimization, quiet hours, reminder lead time, and the task due time.
  • Open the App after a device restart so schedules can be reconciled.
  • Verify the device’s date, time, and timezone.

Android manufacturers may delay background notifications to conserve battery. In-app alerts and task records remain available even when the operating system delays a notification.

Erase local application data

Use More → Privacy, terms & Farm Insights → Privacy & data controls and follow the local-erasure confirmation. This removes the App’s local records, weather caches, copied receipt images, and scheduled reminders.

Export anything you need first. Local erasure cannot be undone, does not remove files you exported elsewhere, and does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

Urgent or safety-critical situations

Support email is not monitored continuously and cannot provide emergency, veterinary, severe-weather, fire, poison-control, equipment-safety, legal, accounting, or tax assistance. Contact the appropriate local emergency service, veterinarian, manufacturer, weather authority, poison-control service, or qualified professional.
