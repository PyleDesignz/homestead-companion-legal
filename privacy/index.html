Privacy Policy

Homestead Companion is designed as a local-first application. This policy explains what information stays on your device and the limited circumstances in which information reaches another service.

1. Who we are

Homestead Companion is an Android application published by PyleDesignz (“we,” “us,” or “our”). Questions about privacy can be sent to james@pyledesignz.com.

2. Scope and launch model

This policy applies to the Homestead Companion mobile application and these public support pages. The initial release is local-first: it does not require a Homestead Companion account, does not synchronize farm records to a PyleDesignz cloud service, and does not include third-party advertising.

These public pages use no analytics, tracking scripts, advertising cookies, or contact forms. Standard infrastructure logs may be processed by GitHub when it delivers these pages under GitHub’s own privacy practices.

3. Information stored locally on your device

You may choose to enter or attach information such as:

  • Profile, household, farm, and farm-location information;
  • Animal, garden bed, crop, harvest, egg, inventory, feed, equipment, maintenance, vendor, task, alert, rainfall, and activity records;
  • Financial ledger entries, costs, income, allocations, notes, and receipt images;
  • Notification preferences, quiet hours, farm colors or icons, units, and other application settings; and
  • Coordinates you enter or select for farm weather.

This information is stored in the application’s private storage on your Android device. PyleDesignz does not operate an application backend that receives these farm records in this release. Receipt images remain in the app’s local document storage unless you choose to share them through Android.

Important: uninstalling the application, clearing its storage, losing the device, or resetting the device can remove local records. Use the application’s export feature and keep your own safe copies.

4. Device permissions and platform features

Location

Location permission is requested only when you choose “Use my current location.” You may enter coordinates manually instead. The app uses the selected coordinates to request U.S. weather information as described below.

Camera and photos

Camera or photo-library access is requested only when you choose to photograph or attach a receipt. The app does not scan your photo library in the background.

Notifications and alarms

If you enable reminders, the app uses Android notification and alarm features to schedule task, inventory, equipment, and weather reminders on your device. Notification permission and exact-alarm access remain under your Android settings.

Files and sharing

When you export records, restore a backup, or share a report, Android’s document picker and sharing interface allow you to choose the destination or source. The selected file provider applies its own privacy practices.

5. External services

The app communicates with a limited number of providers for specific features.

Google Play and RevenueCat

Google Play processes purchases and subscription payments. RevenueCat helps the app retrieve product information and determine whether the pro entitlement is active. These services may process billing-related information such as product identifiers, purchase tokens or receipts, subscription status, an anonymous RevenueCat app-user identifier, and technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot purchases.

PyleDesignz does not receive your full payment-card number. Google and RevenueCat process billing information under their respective policies: Google Privacy Policy and RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

National Weather Service

When you use live weather, the selected farm latitude and longitude and ordinary network metadata are sent directly to api.weather.gov, operated by the U.S. National Weather Service. Names, tasks, inventory, notes, financial entries, and other farm records are not included in the weather request. Live weather currently supports U.S. locations. See the National Weather Service privacy information.

GitHub Pages

GitHub hosts this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use, and the Support page. Visiting these pages may provide GitHub with ordinary web-request data such as an IP address, browser information, and request time under GitHub’s privacy statement.

No advertising or data sale

Homestead Companion contains no third-party advertising SDK. We do not sell your personal information or farm records.

6. User-directed exports and sharing

The app can create JSON and CSV exports. You control whether and where those files are shared. After a file leaves the app, its storage, security, retention, and deletion depend on you and the destination you selected.

JSON backups preserve supported record fields but do not include the underlying receipt image files. Keep original receipts or separate copies if you need them. Restoring a JSON backup validates the selected data and removes receipt-file references that cannot be recreated from the export.

7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you edit or delete them, use the in-app local-data erasure control, clear application storage, or uninstall the app. The app may retain a local previous snapshot and a pre-restore safety snapshot to help recover from an interrupted save or mistaken restore; the in-app erasure control removes the app’s local records, caches, copied receipt images, and scheduled reminders.

Copies you exported to Files, Drive, email, or another destination are outside the app and must be deleted from those locations separately. PyleDesignz cannot remotely retrieve or delete records stored only on your device.

Subscription cancellation and local-data deletion are separate. Erasing local records or uninstalling the app does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Google Play and RevenueCat retain billing records according to legal obligations and their own retention policies.

8. Security

The app relies on Android’s application sandbox and platform security controls for local records. Billing connections and weather requests use HTTPS. Backup imports are size-limited and structurally validated before replacing local data.

No storage method is guaranteed to be perfectly secure or permanent. Protect access to your device, install Android security updates, and maintain backups appropriate for the importance of your records. Avoid sending sensitive records, financial details, or receipt images in an ordinary support email unless they are necessary and you accept the risks of email transmission.

9. Children’s privacy

Homestead Companion is a general farm-management utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly operate an account service that collects children’s personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information to PyleDesignz through support, contact us so we can address it.

10. Your choices

  • Use the app without creating a Homestead Companion account.
  • Enter weather coordinates manually instead of granting location permission.
  • Decline camera, photo, notification, location, or exact-alarm access and continue using features that do not require that permission.
  • Export supported records in JSON or CSV format.
  • Erase local application data through Privacy & data controls.
  • Manage or cancel Pro through Google Play subscriptions.

Because PyleDesignz does not receive or host your local farm records, privacy requests about those records generally must be completed on your device. For billing-related requests, we may ask for the RevenueCat support ID shown in the app. Do not send the contents of your farm records unless necessary.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, its providers, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date will change when we publish a revision. If a future release adds accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, or additional data processing, we will update the disclosures before those features are offered publicly.
