3. Free and Homestead Companion Pro

Free

Free provides one local farm location and the core local recordkeeping, task, activity, weather, financial ledger, export, and restore workflows described in the App. Your core records and supported exports are not withheld because a Pro subscription expires.

Pro

Homestead Companion Pro is an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription. In the launch version, an active Pro entitlement unlocks:

  • Up to five separately managed farm locations;
  • A monthly task calendar with a per-day agenda;
  • Advanced activity search, filters, history ranges, reports, and comparisons;
  • Scheduled device reminders, quiet hours, and snooze controls;
  • Feed runout forecasts and additional farm-weather guidance;
  • Unlimited task-to-inventory automation rules;
  • Exact financial split allocations; and
  • An annual financial category summary and export.

Pro benefits may evolve, but the subscription must continue to provide recurring value. We will not describe cloud sync, household collaboration, or artificial-intelligence services as included until such services are actually available in the production App.

After Pro ends

When Pro expires, Free workflows continue on the primary farm. Existing additional farms, Pro-created records, split allocations, and annual financial records are not deleted and remain included in supported exports. Additional farms become locked against opening, editing, or receiving new records until Pro is restored. Creating new Pro-only automations, reports, allocations, and scheduled Pro reminders stops.
